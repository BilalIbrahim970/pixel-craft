Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Pixel Craft takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name, phone number, email address
  • Project details and requirements
  • Payment information (transaction IDs, not card details)
  • Communication history (WhatsApp, email)

Automatically collected:

  • Browser type, device, operating system
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited and time spent

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide services and process orders
  • Customer support and communication
  • Payment processing and invoicing
  • Service improvements and analytics
  • Marketing (only with your permission)
  • Legal compliance

3. Information Sharing

We never sell your personal information. We only share it in these cases:

  • Service providers: Payment processors (JazzCash, Easypaisa,Bank Transfer)
  • Legal requirements: Court order or legal obligation
  • Business transfer: If the business is sold
  • With consent: With your explicit permission

4. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures:

  • Encrypted communication (Email,WhatsApp)
  • Limited access — only authorized team members
  • Regular security reviews
  • We do not store payment data (third-party processors are used)

Note: No one can guarantee 100% security on the internet. We make our best effort but cannot promise absolute security.

5. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies:

  • Essential: To save theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Analytics: Anonymous traffic data (if enabled)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • To access your information
  • To correct inaccurate data
  • To request data deletion
  • To opt out of marketing communications
  • To request data portability

For requests, email contact.pixelcraaft@gmail.com.

7. Data Retention

  • Project files: Deleted 1 year after delivery
  • Customer records: 3 years (tax/legal requirements)
  • Communication: 2 years

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain third-party links (WhatsApp, social media). We do not control their privacy policies — please check them separately.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. International Users

If you are outside Pakistan, your data will be processed in Pakistan. By placing an order, you agree to this transfer.

11. Changes to Policy

This policy may be updated. Notification of material changes will appear on the website. Continued use means you agree to the new policy.
