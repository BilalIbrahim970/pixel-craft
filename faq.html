Frequently Asked Questions

The questions everyone asks — all answered here

How does the process start?

Simple 3 steps: (1) Fill the order form or message us on WhatsApp, (2) We discuss your requirements and provide a quote, (3) Work begins after a 50% advance payment.

How do I make the payment?

We accept JazzCash, Easypaisa, and Bank Transfer. Standard: 50% advance, 50% on delivery. Custom payment plans are available for bulk orders.

How long does delivery take?

Depends on the service:
• Logo Design: 2–3 days
• Video Editing: 1–2 days
• Web Development: 5–10 days
• SEO & Social Media: Monthly retainer

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! Logo Design includes unlimited revisions until you're satisfied. Other services include 2–3 standard revisions, with extra revisions available for a small fee.

Do you offer refunds?

Advance payments are non-refundable once work has begun. However, if we miss a deadline or fail to deliver quality, a partial or full refund may be issued. See our Refund Policy for details.

Who owns the final files?

After full payment, all files and rights belong to you. We may showcase the work in our portfolio (with your permission), but we will never resell it.

Do you accept international clients?

Absolutely! We work worldwide. International clients can pay via PayPal or Wise. We also provide quotes in USD.

How will we communicate?

Primary: WhatsApp (fastest). For long projects, we also send email updates. Daily progress reports available on request.

Do I get the source files?

Yes — SVG, AI, EPS for logos; project files for videos; full source code for websites. Everything is handed over to you.

What if I have more questions?

Just message us on WhatsApp — you'll get a reply within 5 minutes.

Still have questions?

Talk to us directly on WhatsApp — we'll clear everything up.
