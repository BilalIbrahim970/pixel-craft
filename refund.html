Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Pixel Craft follows a fair and transparent refund policy. Below are the details of when and how a refund may be issued.

1. General Policy

Standard rule: Advance payment is non-refundable once work has begun. However, partial or full refunds are issued in certain situations (see below).

2. Refund Eligible Cases

Full Refund

  • Work has not started at all (cancelled within 24 hours of advance)
  • Pixel Craft was unable to start the project (due to our fault)
  • Duplicate payment was made

Partial Refund (50%)

  • Work has begun but less than 25% is complete
  • Customer cancels for personal reasons (early stage)

Partial Refund (25%)

  • Work is 25–50% complete
  • Cancelled midway

No Refund

  • Work is more than 50% complete
  • Final delivery has been made
  • Customer approved and later wants changes
  • Customer provided copyrighted/illegal content
  • Customer failed to give clear instructions, causing the delay themselves
  • Monthly services (SEO, Social Media) — for the month the work has begun

3. Deadline Miss Policy

If we miss our committed deadline:

  • 1–3 days late: 10% refund or discount on next order
  • 4–7 days late: 25% refund
  • 7+ days late: 50% refund + full delivery (if customer wishes)

Note: The deadline may be extended if the customer delays feedback.

4. Quality Dispute

If there is disagreement on delivery quality:

  1. Customer lists specific issues on WhatsApp
  2. We offer revisions (first)
  3. If still unsatisfied after 3 revisions, a partial refund (max 50%)
  4. If we are unable to deliver, a full refund

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Message on WhatsApp: +92 331 4440854
  2. Provide order details and the reason for the refund
  3. We will review within 48 hours
  4. Once approved, the refund is processed within 5–7 business days

6. Refund Method

Refunds are issued via the same method used for payment:

  • JazzCash / Easypaisa: To the same number
  • Bank Transfer: To the same account
  • PayPal: To the PayPal account (5–10 days)
  • Refund transaction charges are borne by the customer (if applicable)

7. Non-Refundable Items

  • Third-party costs (domain, hosting, premium plugins)
  • Rush/emergency surcharge
  • Already-delivered files
  • Consultation charges

8. Chargeback Policy

Please contact us before filing a direct chargeback. Filing a chargeback without contacting us first may result in:

  • Future services being denied
  • Possible legal action

We prefer to resolve issues directly first.

9. Cancellation by Pixel Craft

If we cancel a project for any reason:

  • Complete refund within 48 hours
  • Completed work (if any) handed over free of cost
  • No penalty to the customer

10. Special Cases

In certain special cases (medical emergency, natural disaster, etc.), we may be extra flexible. Contact us on WhatsApp.

11. Agreement

By placing an order, you agree to this refund policy. The policy may be updated at any time. The policy applicable at the time of order is the one that applies.

12. Contact
